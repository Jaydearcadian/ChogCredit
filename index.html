Transferability

Ownership and debt must move safely together.

The prototype allows you to inspect transfer states, but does not perform an NFT transfer. Debt-bearing transfers require buyer acceptance and atomic lender-protective checks.

Credit facility

Borrow, without unwinding.

Borrowing capacity is derived from collateral value and a conservative maximum loan-to-value limit. This demo uses a simple illustrative rule, not a live risk engine.
